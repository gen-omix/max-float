# ui

The page: one screen of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step. The service serves it at `/` and at `/{key}`. Words on the page are the words in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md). What the page reads and writes is in [graph/README.md](../graph/README.md).

## Layout

Tabs across the top: **Team**, one tab for each applicant with their name, and **+ Add your name**, where typing a name adds an applicant. Whoever just made the team starts on their own tab, as does anyone this browser knows is in the team. Anyone else is asked who they are.

An applicant's tab has their ratings, for all rivers and for one river, their unavailable dates, the launches they apply for in the team's plan, and Done.

The Team tab has the rivers its applicants chose and a trip length for each, `minimum`, everyone's unavailable dates on one calendar with how many are away each day, the shares of the applicants' ratings averaged, shown but not set there, and the team's plan, which keeps itself up to date: each applicant's application in each lottery. Along the bottom, the lock.

Typing the first name on `/` makes the party and moves to `/{key}`. The address is the link to share. Each tab saves itself a few seconds after an edit and when it's closed, and the team's latest plan is saved with it.

## Preferences

| Preference | Control | Starts at |
|---|---|---|
| Flow | No, OK, Good or Ideal for each of not running · extra low · low · normal · high · spicy high · extra spicy high | blank |
| Temperature | No, OK, Good or Ideal for each of cold · cool · mild · warm · hot · extra hot | blank |
| Applicants who must be free | for each river, how many of its applicants must be free for a launch, from 1 to the whole team, with a **?** that explains it on hover, focus or a tap | 1 |
| Priority | Not, Some, More or Most for each of better historical permit odds, flow and temperatures | blank |

Each row's share of its set is its rating, 0 to 3, over the set's total, so changing one row never moves another. Nothing starts rated, so every team's preferences are its own.

The ratings decide which launches can be in the plan: a category rated No removes every launch that is typically in it. Among the launches left, a bigger share for a launch's categories in past years makes a better match, and the priority then weighs odds, flow and temperature.

### For one river

Each river in the river list opens to show the same controls. Changing one there sets it for that river only, and that river keeps it when the party's changes. So a river's flow ratings or priority replace the party's for that river, while its other ratings can still follow the party. A river's priority changes which launches that river gets, never how many. A river with any preference of its own stays open, so a difference is never hidden behind a closed river. Setting a river's controls back to the party's values closes it again.

## Results

- First, when a river was widened: a red banner across the full width of the results, one line per river, such as "Salt: no launches matched. Widened flow to include low." It can't be dismissed, and it goes away when the river no longer needs widening.
- Next: the odds of at least one permit, against the most common launches and random ones, as in v0.3.
- Then one row per launch: date, river, the applicants applying for it, odds, typical flow and typical temperature. The whole team applies for the same launches unless someone isn't free for one. A widened river's rows are marked "widened" in red.
- Once everyone is done, the lock saves the plan as the team's applications and locks them, and the bar at the top shows when, with **Unlock**. Nothing can be changed until the team unlocks them. Applications count against other parties only once they're locked in, one for each applicant applying for a launch.

### Widened rivers

A widened river stays open, and the categories added for it show in red in its ratings, and each counts as OK beside the river's Ideal. They aren't saved as ratings: the party's own ratings stay as set, and the widening is worked out again each time the plan updates. The banner also covers a river with no launches to widen to, such as "Salt: no launches when the party is free."

### Below the whole party

The minimum starts at one, so the team's plan can use a launch some of a river's applicants can't make. The applicants it names for that launch are the ones who can, and the others get the best launches they're free for. With the minimum at everyone applying for a river, every launch in its plan is one they're all free for.
